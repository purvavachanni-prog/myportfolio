A cafe reservation management website developed to simplify the reservation process and provide users with an organized booking experience.

**Key Features:**

- Displays reservation history.
- Provides an option to cancel existing reservations.
- Offers an interactive and user-friendly interface.

### 2. Craftverse

**Project Overview:** Online Marketplace for Artisans

Craftverse is a web-based platform designed to provide artisans with an online space to showcase and sell their handmade products directly to customers.

**Key Objectives:**
- Provide artisans with digital visibility.
- Showcase handmade and creative products.
- Connect artisans with potential customers.
- Support the promotion and sale of handmade products through an online marketplace.

### 3. Campus Guardian

**Technology:** Amazon Web Services (AWS), Multi-Agent AI

Campus Guardian is an AWS-powered multi-agent AI assistant designed to help college students stay informed and organized throughout their academic journey.

**Key Features:**
- Provides updates about college events and activities.
- Includes a Learning Management System (LMS).
- Helps students track project submissions.
- Keeps students informed about assignment deadlines.
- Uses a multi-agent AI approach to support different student requirements.
